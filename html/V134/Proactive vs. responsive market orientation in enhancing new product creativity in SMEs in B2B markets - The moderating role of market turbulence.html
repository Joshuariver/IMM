Proactive vs. responsive market orientation in enhancing new product creativity in SMEs in B2B markets:
The moderating role of market turbulence

Ho-Taek Yi, Guihan Ko, Michael Obal

B2B 시장 중소기업(SME)의 신제품 창의성 향상에서 선제적 대 반응적 시장 지향성:
시장 격동성의 조절 효과

Translation

Original Abstract (EN)

"This study aims to identify the antecedents of new product (NP) creativity of SMEs in the B2B industry and their relationship with NP performance based on the SPP (Sources of Advantage – Positional Advantage – Performance Outcome) Framework. Particularly, this study distinguishes market orientation into proactive market orientation and responsive market orientation to identify their differential effects on NP creativity. It also investigates the moderating effect of market turbulence relationship between market orientation and NP creativity. A survey was conducted with 313 B2B SMEs, and the hypotheses regarding the relationships between market orientation, NP creativity were tested. The results supported all hypotheses except for the effect of responsive market orientation on novelty. Further, proactive market orientation (PMO) was shown to have a stronger influence on developing novel products while responsive market orientation (RMO) had a stronger influence on the development of meaningful products. Additionally, the findings suggest that under conditions of higher market turbulence, responsive market orientation (RMO) may lead to greater improvements in both new product novelty and meaningfulness. This study provides academic and practical implications by highlighting the differential impacts of the two types of market orientation on new product creativity and the moderating role of market turbulence."

Research Framework (가설 경로 모형)

반응적 시장 지향성 (RMO)

개념 정의: 고객사가 현재 겉으로 표현하는 명시적 요구사항을 기민하게 받아들이려는 지향성.

측정 문항: Narver et al. (2004) 4문항 (Cronbach's α = 0.850)

신제품 독창성 (Novelty)

개념 정의: 개발된 신제품이 기존 대안들과 비교해 참신하고 radical한 차별성을 가지는 정도.

측정 문항: Im & Workman (2004) 5문항 (Cronbach's α = 0.947)

신제품 의미성 (Meaningfulness)

개념 정의: 신제품이 고객사의 실제 니즈에 적합하고 실용적으로 작동해 이득을 제공하는 정도.

측정 문항: Yi et al. (2021) 5문항 (Cronbach's α = 0.949)
